Drawn, then
made twice.

  1. Step 1

    Full-scale elevation

    Every piece is drawn at 1:1 on the shop wall. If a curve looks wrong at life size it is wrong, and no rendering will tell you that.

  2. Step 2

    A prototype nobody sells

    The first build gets sat in for six weeks by whoever is in the workshop, then taken apart to see which joint moved.

  3. Step 3

    The one you get

    Cut from the corrected drawing, stamped with its sheet number and the initials of the person who assembled it.
